Vacation Scheduler Mobile App

About the App

The Vacation Scheduler Mobile App is your go-to solution for planning and managing vacations and excursions with ease. Designed for Android 8.0+ devices, this app allows travelers to organize vacation details like dates, hotels, and activities, set reminders, and share plans—all in one place. With offline functionality powered by Room Database, you can access your plans anytime, anywhere. Perfect for casual vacationers, busy professionals, and families looking to streamline their travel planning.
